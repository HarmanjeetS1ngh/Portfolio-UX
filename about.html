Where I Started

I Loved Figuring Out How Computers Work

I chose Computer Science to understand how technology works behind the screen. I spent my early college years exploring code and security.

The Realization

Code is Nothing Without the People Using It

I realized great code alone isn't enough. If people struggle to use a product, it fails. That's when my focus shifted to the user.

Changing Paths

Teaching Myself How to Solve Human Problems

Without a design school background, I built my own path through books, courses, and studying great digital experiences.

Telling Stories

Learning Empathy Through a Camera Lens

Freelance video editing taught me about emotion, attention, and storytelling. Every experience is a journey for someone.

The Destination

Connecting the Dots to UX Design

My technical background, curiosity about people, and love for storytelling naturally led me to UX design.
